# CFW v14: no stock launcher while a phone is linked

The RayNeo launcher's home showed whenever the Glassly page was not up: after
a connect until the phone opened the page, after the page lease ran out, when
a crown press held past 2 s closed the page on the glasses, and when the phone
asked for the stock dashboard. Wearers then saw RayNeo's app carousel, whose
apps cannot run without the RayNeo app.

`ViewManager::onEnterState` hides every view, then shows the one its state
names. v14 retargets its two `show` calls for the launcher home:

| Site | Stock call | Now |
|---|---|---|
| `0x107A1B76` | `AppListView::show` (`0x1079A785`) | `cfw_applist_show` |
| `0x107A1AB4` | `BoardView::show` (`0x1083BAC5`) | `cfw_board_show` |

Both skip the stock `show` while `LinkMonitor` reports the phone connected
(`MonitorManager::getLinkMonitor` → byte `+6`, the state
`LinkMonitor::notifyConnectionChange` stores), so the panel stays black until
the Glassly page takes it. The app list's crown-wheel listener is registered
by `show`, so it stays off too. With no phone linked both show as stock; the
disconnected, setup and pairing views are other states and other views and
are untouched.

The local long-press close (`LONG_CLOSE_MS`, `PAGE_USER`) is gone: a long
press is only a gesture for the phone. A phone that goes away still loses the
page when its lease runs out.

The phone side (Glassly `845c081784`) holds the page from detection, raises it
again whatever closed it, and no longer closes it for the stock dashboard.

## Validation

- Firmware cross-build, 76 symbols resolved by name and verified, both hook
  sites byte-checked as the expected `bl`, patch replay/reproduction and OTA
  integrity checks pass; the patched shims disassemble to the gate above.
- Host suites (cache, compass, head, image, memory, raster, vector) and the
  Python protocol tests pass.

Hardware validation is pending: flash v14, connect, and check that the panel
stays black (never the app carousel) before the page opens, after closing the
dashboard, after a long crown press, and after the phone app is killed. Then
turn the phone's Bluetooth off: the disconnected view must show. Re-pair
(fold, hold 5 s): the pairing view must show.
